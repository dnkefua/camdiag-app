CamDiag Terms of Use

Last updated: 15 July 2026

Clinical limitation: CamDiag is an investigational clinical decision-support tool. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional judgment. Do not use it for emergencies. Every transcription and AI-assisted finding must be verified by a qualified clinician.

Authorized use

Use CamDiag only when you are authorized to process the relevant health information and only for legitimate care or approved evaluation purposes.

Human review

You are responsible for checking source documents, OCR text, medication names, doses, units, allergies, interactions, and all outputs before any decision.

Prohibited use

Do not use CamDiag as an autonomous diagnostic or prescribing system, upload unnecessary identifiers, attempt to bypass security controls, or use outputs to delay emergency care.

Availability

Services may be unavailable or produce incomplete results. Preserve access to normal clinical workflows.

Contact

Contact NDN Analytics through ndnanalytics.com for legal, privacy, or safety questions.
