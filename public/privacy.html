CamDiag Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026

CamDiag is an AI-assisted clinical decision-support tool for healthcare workers. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace clinician judgment. Emergency warning signs require immediate care.

Information We Process

CamDiag may process account information, medical document images, AI-assisted review outputs, medication safety queries, app usage events, and security audit records needed to provide and protect the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users, provide clinical document review, support medication safety checks, enforce rate limits, detect abuse, improve reliability, and maintain audit logs for safety and security.

Medical Data

Users should upload only information necessary for clinical review. AI-assisted outputs must be reviewed by qualified healthcare professionals before any clinical decision.

Sharing

We do not sell personal or health data. Data may be processed by service providers used to run the app, including Firebase, Google Cloud, and Google AI services, under applicable platform terms and security controls.

Retention and Deletion

Patient records and scan records are scoped to the signed-in user. Users may request deletion of account data or operational records by contacting NDN Analytics.

Security

CamDiag uses Firebase Authentication, Firestore security rules, backend-only AI access, App Check wiring, CORS restrictions, and audit/rate-limit controls to protect the service.
